Stumbled? Backspace rewinds to the start of the sentence you are on: just say it again and keep going (↑ goes back one more sentence). Enter stops and saves · Esc discards the take · P plays the saved take · ←/→ change scene.

Read at a natural pace; the silence before your first word and after your last is trimmed automatically. Keep the total under 15:00 (bottom left).
